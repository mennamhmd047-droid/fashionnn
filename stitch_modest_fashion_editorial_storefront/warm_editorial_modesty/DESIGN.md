## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury, modern editorial e-commerce sanctuary dedicated to contemporary modest and hijabi fashion. Tailored for stylish, self-assured young women seeking graceful expression, the aesthetic balances high-fashion editorial prestige with soft, intimate warmth.

The visual movement blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous, breathable negative space reminiscent of print luxury lookbooks.
- Tactile, gentle pill geometries contrasting crisp typography.
- Bilingual typographical poise ensuring seamless optical parity between Latin and Arabic scripts.
- A discreet, elevated tone that refrains from aggressive sales patterns in favor of artful curations, modest styling stories, and serene textural layers.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from fine textiles—raw silks, washed linens, and grounded earthen pigments.

- **Primary (`#2A2121` Deep Espresso):** Replaces harsh standard blacks. Used for primary narrative typography, high-priority actions, structured active states, and focal icons. Imparts grounding, structure, and editorial weight.
- **Secondary (`#DDA79F` Dusty Rose / Blush):** Used for elevated accents, selected state fills, promotional editorial ribbons, and curated styling touchpoints. Works harmoniously with soft blush tones (`#E8CFC8`).
- **Tertiary (`#8E9E8C` Muted Sage):** Evokes serenity and organic textile craft. Assigned to seasonal tags, sustainability identifiers, fabric highlights, and secondary status badges.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2` Alabaster Cream):** The canvas background. Accompanied by `#F4EFEB` for secondary surfaces, container wells, and card backdrops to ensure soft visual containment without sterile white glare.
- **Neutral Accent (`#A3968E` Warm Taupe):** Secondary copy, hairline borders, unselected pagination, and fabric spec indicators.

## Typography

The typographical pairing relies on contrast between high-fashion literary heritage and crisp, accessible geometry.

- **Display & Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys timeless couture refinement. High-contrast vertical stems and delicate serifs bring editorial authority to hero statements, seasonal lookbooks, and collection titles.
- **Interface & Narrative Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances modern functionality with warm, open counters. Its geometric cleanliness provides optimal legibility for product descriptions, filtering taxonomy, and transactional checkout steps.
- **Bilingual English/Arabic Execution:** When switching to Arabic, headline serif treatments map seamlessly to an equivalent high-contrast modern Naskh/Thuluth-inspired editorial cut, while Plus Jakarta Sans mirrors modern Arabic rounded geometric sans-serifs. Maintain line-height multipliers with an additional 10–15% breathing room in Arabic body copy to accommodate vertical ascenders and diacritics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a responsive fluid grid system characterized by expansive margins and deliberate asymmetric pacing.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid, max container width of 1440px with `margin: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid, `margin: 2rem` (32px), `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px).
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` (20px), `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical stacking (`space-xl` to `80px` section gaps) ensures product images command full visual contemplation without cramped commercial tension.
- **Horizontal Carousels:** On mobile, filter pills and lookbook capsules bleed past right margins (or left margins in RTL Arabic orientation) using dynamic edge padding, encouraging smooth horizontal gesture navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects sharp corporate drop shadows in favor of warm, diffuse luminosity, creating an ethereal, layered canvas:

- **Ambient Warm Shadows:** All elevation employs a soft espresso/taupe tint rather than pure grey/black.
  - *Floating Quick-Add / Action Bars:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(42, 33, 33, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(42, 33, 33, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating Modal / Mini Cart:* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(42, 33, 33, 0.12)`.
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is primarily established through surface transitions: `#FAF7F2` (canvas base) stacked under `#F4EFEB` (card and drawer containers).
- **Hairline Ghost Borders:** Subtle structure is maintained via 1px border lines in `#EFE8DE` or `#A3968E` at 15% opacity, preserving clean boundaries in bright sunlight or low-contrast screen conditions.
- **Backdrop Blurs:** Navigation headers and sticky cart pills utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over 85% translucent `#FAF7F2` surface fills.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an organic, gentle geometry that mirrors modest silhouettes and draped fabrics.

- **Base Radius (Roundedness 2):** Primary cards, interactive panels, dropdowns, and input containers employ smooth roundedness (`16px` to `24px`).
- **Pill Philosophy:** Interactive controls—including buttons, filter badges, color swatch highlights, and quantity adjustments—strictly adopt full pill shapes (`9999px`), delivering a tactile, feminine touchpoint.
- **Image Aspect Ratios:** Product photography strictly adheres to a tall editorial 3:4 or 4:5 portrait ratio, softened with `rounded-lg` (16px) corner radiuses to seamlessly fuse imagery with the container surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Pill Action:** Deep espresso fill (`#2A2121`), crisp cream text (`#FAF7F2`), full pill radius (`9999px`), vertical padding `space-md`, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Hover brings subtle expansion with a soft blush tint overlay.
- **Secondary Pill Action:** Transparent base, 1px espresso hairline border, deep espresso text.
- **Floating Quick-Add:** An icon or compact pill button positioned at the bottom right of product cards. Displays on image hover on desktop; anchored seamlessly on touch devices. Accompanied by diffuse warm ambient elevation.

### Product Cards & Swatches
- **Container:** Borderless card on `#F4EFEB` background with `rounded-lg` (16px) corners. 
- **Imagery:** Draped lookbook photography at 3:4 portrait ratio.
- **Color Swatches:** Centered or left-aligned circular dots (18px diameter) framed by a 2px offset border ring on active selection, highlighting natural modal, silk, and chiffon fabric colorways.
- **Badging:** Floating micro-pills at top edge using sage (`#8E9E8C`) for "Organic Chiffon" or dusty rose (`#DDA79F`) for "Limited Drop".

### Filter Pill Carousel
- Horizontally scrollable row of pill-shaped tags.
- **Default State:** Alabaster fill, subtle 1px border in `#A3968E` (20% opacity), `label-md` typography.
- **Active State:** Deep espresso fill (`#2A2121`) with cream text (`#FAF7F2`), eliminating the need for rigid checkboxes in catalog views.

### "Complete the Look" (Hijabi Styling Module)
- Curated modular card grouping dresses/abayas with matched hijabs, undercaps, and magnetic pins.
- Horizontal layout pairing an editorial outfit snapshot with child product pills that feature instant single-tap accessory add-ons.
- Accented with a soft dusty rose hairline container (`#E8CFC8`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Soft alabaster fill (`#FAF7F2`), 1.5px border in warm taupe (`#A3968E` at 30%), pill or 16px corner radius, floating label transitions in `label-sm`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic pill selections or soft circular radios filled with deep espresso and dust rose check marks.

### Minimal Header & Navigation
- Heightened vertical whitespace with centered editorial logo mark in Playfair Display.
- Utility cluster: minimal line-weight search, localized language toggle (EN/AR), saved wishlist heart, and floating count badge on the bag icon in dusty rose (`#DDA79F`).
